PUPPY TAILS

Where they are now.

Photos and puppy tails from our past litters — sent in by the families who took them home.

Sugar & Sonic’s Candy Litter

Born April 22, 2025 · Australian Bernedoodles

Lolli/Stella

Lolli was Sugar’s only reddish-blond pup, making her Sugar’s clone. She was so much fun!

Stella/Lolli

Stella resides in Northern California where she is the social butterfly of the community. She frequents the coffee shops and is always ready for a hike.

Oreo/Oreo

Oreo was so fun to raise. He was one of a kind in the litter — the only black and white doodle. As time went on, we found out he looked just like his grandfather.

Oreo/Oreo

Oreo resides in the bay area with his great parents! They absolutely adore him!

Kisses/Zuri

Kisses is such a beautiful tri-merle! Her white markings are just stunning! She was just a sweetheart!

Zuri/Kisses

Zuri resides in a neighboring town with Cachi/Cocoa from one of Ginger’s first litter. They both have wonderful human parents!

Milky/Gerald

Milky is such a sweet boy! He was always everyone’s favorite. He had the most calm personality.

Gerald/Milky

Gerald resides in Fresno with a great family! They run a daycare in their home and Gerald is a welcome friend to all of the kids.

Snickers/Gizmo

Snickers was our little runt! There were a few times we did not know he would make it. But oh how he fought! He went from being a little tiny weakling to a very handsome boy!

Gizmo/Snickers

Gizmo makes his home close by with a wonderful family. He has lots of kids and his new canine sister Xannie as part of his family. He is very much adored!

Skittle/Goldie

Skittle is a beautiful apricot parti! Her coloring is so similar to her mom’s, just with more white. She was so spunky and smart!

Goldie/Skittle

Goldie resides in the south valley where she just graduated as a cardiac alert dog. We are so impressed with this sweetheart!

Hershey/Burt

Hershey was our only puppy that looks like a Bernese Mountain Dog. He is beautiful! He is on the smaller side but so spunky!

Burt/Hershey

Burt lives in State College, Pennsylvania. He resides with our son and will be used as a stud. Burt has a great life as he goes to work every day with his human dad. But work is so fun as it is on a baseball field! Burt rides on mowers, dodges water spraying from hoses and runs until he poops out! He does all of this with his big canine brother Chief, a stunning white Akita.

Reese/Sky

Reese was the cutest, spunkiest little thing. With her beautiful apricot parti coat and her willingness to bounce all over, one word comes to mind — FUN!

Sky/Reese

Reese only had to move two doors down to find her special people! She is now Sky and has her perfect playmate, her human sister! It was a match made in heaven!

Crunch/Leo

Everyone ooohed and aaahed when Crunch was born. His colors were absolutely stunning! Not to mention how cute he was!

Leo/Crunch

Crunch is now Leo and he lives with his wonderful family on a ranch in Colorado. Leo is living his dream!

Rolo/Happy

Rolo was so fluffy. His gorgeous apricot parti coat made a big ball of fluff. He was always one of the larger of the litter but never a bully.

Happy/Rolo

Rolo is now Happy! Happy lives in Fresno with his human brother and sister. I am not sure if Happy loves his human siblings more or if they love him more. Quite mutual!

Kit/Maisy

Kit was one of our apricot partis and looked most like a spaniel. She was beautiful with her long ears!

Maisy/Kit

Kit is now Maisy and lives with her wonderful family in central California. Maisy’s whole family is awesome but her special bond is with her human sister.

Tootsie/Stormy

Last but not least is Tootsie. Tootsie was one of our tiniest puppies. Such a cute tri merle. She was tiny but always right in the middle of everything.

Stormy/Tootsie

Tootsie is now Stormy and still lives with us, with her mom and grandma. She belongs to our son. There is a possibility we could breed her at some point. She is the cutest, and personality plus!

Ginger & Sonic

Born April 17, 2025 · Australian Bernedoodles

Trump/Handsome Harvey

Trump was such a delight to raise. His love for water was undeniable at a very young age. He was always the first to empty the water pan.

Handsome Harvey/Trump

Handsome Harvey wears his name very well! Harvey resides in southern California with his very devoted family. He gets to go to work with his mom, out for walks, and all the fun of sunny California.

Maga/Joey

Maga was always such a joy! Always so well behaved!

Joey/Maga

When Joey’s mom came to pick her puppy out, it only took her at most 30 seconds. She looked at Joey and said that is the one! She told him what kind of life he would have and there was an instant connection! And he does have a wonderful life on the Central Coast, filled with adventure. Joey’s mom says Joey is pure love!

Kennedy/Lucky

Kennedy gave us so many laughs! She was actually an escape artist! Out of 23 puppies, she is the only one who would try and succeed at getting out of her pen. So much so that my grandson, Cooper, built a double gate like at the zoo. You can imagine her surprise when she was trapped. Other than that, she always wanted in my lap. Just loved her!

Lucky/Kennedy

Lucky resides in Fresno with her human parents. Her human mom says she is very smart and has the best personality. She loves her walks!

Liberty/Abbie

Liberty has the perfect coloring and coat! She has white in all the right places and just enough curl. Love this little gal!

Abbie/Liberty

Abbie’s home is in Southern California in a retirement community. Her yard butts up to the walking path; hence, Abbie greets everyone who comes by.

Caroline/Mollie

Caroline was a dream pup to raise! She was always so sweet. She was very mellow but would also stand her ground with her larger siblings.

Mollie/Caroline

Mollie lives all the way in Virginia with her great human parents. Mollie also lives with her half sister Noel. They have a great time together. Also Mollie’s half brother Tiberius lives very close and they also get to play!

Justice/Charlie

Justice was the very first puppy born! We thought he was just beautiful with his tri-colored merle coat.

Charlie/Justice

Charlie lives with a dog-loving human family in the Bay area. He has quite a few canine siblings — I think he has one of every color. Charlie has a very fun home! There is a possibility he will be trained as a service dog.

Vance/Miles

Vance had all the good qualities! He was a beautiful red, perfect white markings, very calm, and just as sweet as could be!

Miles/Vance

Miles resides in Northern California in a guardian home. He has a really fun human mom. Miles will be part of the breeding program for Riverbend Doodles. We can’t wait to see his offspring!

Doge/Bo

Isn’t Doge just the cutest? He has the best coat, it is so soft and fluffy! Doge was our water puppy — he emptied the water dish daily!

Bo/Doge

Bo lives in Fresno with his great human family and his half brother Bentley. As soon as I knew that Doge was a water dog, I reached out to Bentley’s family as Bentley is also a water dog. They absolutely love the water just like their mom! Bo and Bentley are always going for a swim. This picture describes it all!

Baron/Barron

Baron was very much a favorite amongst all the kids! He was a very good puppy. He was going to be pretty big.

Barron/Baron

Barron lives in a small farm town with a family with human siblings that play outside with him all the time. Barron is very good with all the kids! His human dad says he wants to be a lap dog!

Melania/Ginger

Melania has it all! She is beautiful! Melania is a clone of her mom — of all the pups, she looked just like her. Melania had the sweetest temperament and was always a favorite!

Ginger/Melania

Melania is now Ginger. She has her mom’s name; so fitting! Ginger resides in the Sacramento area with her human mom and sister. They love her and say that she is family now!

Noel

Noel

Carmel/Dougie

Dougie/Carmel

Bell/Bella

Bella/Bell

Bella was the very first puppy to find her forever home. I believe it was love at first sight for her forever family.

Cocoa/Cachi

Cocoa was the very first puppy born!

Cachi/Cocoa

Not much has changed — Cachi is still as handsome as ever.

Cookie/Bentley
