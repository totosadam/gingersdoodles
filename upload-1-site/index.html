WHY GINGER’S DOODLES

A small breeder who knows every puppy by name

Four things we will not compromise on — and the promise behind each one.

Raised in our home

Our dogs are family first. Puppies are born and raised underfoot, handled daily, and used to family life long before they leave.

Health first

Genetically health-tested parents. Every puppy is vet-examined, dewormed and started on vaccinations before go-home day.

Allergy-friendly coats

Australian Labradoodles and Bernedoodles carry low-shedding, low-dander fleece and wool coats — a relief for sensitive families.

Support for life

We are always a call or a message away — for the first hard night, the first haircut, and every year after.

MEET THE BREEDER

Hi — I'm the heart behind Ginger's Doodles

Raising doodles has become one of the greatest joys of my life. Every litter has deepened my love for raising exceptional puppies and connecting families with the right lifelong companion.

Ginger's Doodles is a family endeavor. My family is actively involved in raising and socializing every puppy, making sure each one gets daily love, attention, and positive experiences from the very beginning.

My mission is to raise healthy, confident, well-rounded family companions with stable temperaments and a strong foundation for lifelong success — Early Neurological Stimulation, age-appropriate enrichment, emotional resilience, and positive socialization through each puppy's critical developmental stages.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

All questions →
How do I reserve a puppy?
Start with the puppy application. Once we have talked, a reservation fee secures your pick spot on the waitlist for an upcoming litter and is applied to your puppy’s total.
When can my puppy come home?
Puppies go home around eight weeks, once they are weaned, vet-checked, and have started their vaccinations.
How do you choose which puppy is ours?
Selection happens after temperament evaluations at about seven weeks. Waitlist families pick in order, and we help match personality to your household.
Are these dogs good with kids and allergies?
Wonderfully so. Their low-shedding, low-dander coats are also a great fit for many households with mild allergies.
