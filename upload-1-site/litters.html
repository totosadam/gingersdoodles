HOW THE WAITLIST WORKS

A short list, in order, with plenty of contact

1 · Apply

Send in the puppy application. We read every one and reply personally, usually within a few days.

2 · Talk it through

A call or a long message thread. We want to know your household, and you should ask us anything.

3 · Reserve your spot

A reservation fee holds your pick spot for an upcoming litter and is applied to your puppy’s total.

4 · Choose your puppy

After temperament evaluations at around seven weeks, waitlist families select in order. We will help you match a temperament to your household — and we very much want to hear what you are hoping for.
