MEET THE BREEDER

Hi — I’m the heart behind Ginger’s Doodles

Lana, and a family that is in it with me: raising healthy, confident, well-rounded companions in a home where puppies are never far from someone’s lap.

Lana GINGER'S DOODLES

Raising doodles has become one of the greatest joys of my life. Every litter has deepened my love for raising exceptional puppies and connecting families with the right lifelong companion. I genuinely love meeting new people and helping them find the puppy that fits their home, their lifestyle, and their heart.

Ginger’s Doodles is a family endeavor. My family is actively involved in raising and socializing every puppy, making sure each one gets daily love, attention, and positive experiences from the very beginning.

My mission is to raise healthy, confident, well-rounded family companions with stable temperaments and a strong foundation for lifelong success — Early Neurological Stimulation, age-appropriate enrichment, emotional resilience, and positive socialization through each puppy’s critical developmental stages.

To me, breeding is about far more than producing beautiful puppies. It’s about giving each one the very best start in life and supporting families long after they bring their new best friend home.
