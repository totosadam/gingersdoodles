How to edit

  • Text: click any words on the page and type.
  • Photo: click a photo, pick a new one from your computer.
  • Boxes (puppies, litters, FAQ, gallery photos): click inside one, then use the blue bar to add a copy, move, delete, or change the status.
  • Links & buttons: click the text, press Link, type the address.
  • Google title: the “Google title & description” button.
  • Same word everywhere: Find & replace.
  • Go live: press Save & publish. The site updates in about a minute.
  • Made a mistake? Undo, or Backups to go back.

Your work is also kept as a draft in this browser until you publish.
