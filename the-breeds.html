THE TWO BREEDS WE RAISE

Which doodle suits your family?

Both are allergy-friendly, people-oriented and wonderful with children. The difference is mostly in the flavor of their personality.

Australian Labradoodle

Bred for decades as therapy and assistance dogs, they are intuitive, biddable, and eager to be wherever you are. Soft fleece or wool coats, minimal shedding, and a steady off-switch at home.

TemperamentGentle, people-focused
CoatFleece or wool, low shed
EnergyModerate, settles indoors
Sizes we raiseMini & medium

Australian Bernedoodle

The Bernese heart — affectionate, comic, and famous for the lean-into-your-legs cuddle — with an Australian Labradoodle’s coat and trainability behind it. Wonderful with children.

TemperamentDevoted, playful, calm
CoatCurly, low shed
EnergyEasygoing, loves company
Sizes we raiseMini & medium

Grooming, honestly

Low shedding means the coat keeps growing. Plan on brushing a few times a week and a professional groom every eight to ten weeks. We will show you how before you leave.

Allergies

No dog is truly hypoallergenic, but these coats shed very little dander, and most sensitive families do beautifully with them. Come visit and see for yourself.

Sizes

We raise mini and medium doodles — roughly 20 to 45 lb grown. We will give you an honest weight estimate based on the pairing, not a wishful one.

Not sure which one fits?

Tell us about your household in the application and we will tell you honestly which breed and which pup we would put with you.
